How it works

The Counter runs an adaptive, margin-weighted Elo engine replayed across every NFL game since 1999, adjusted game-by-game for starting quarterback efficiency, defensive strength, and draft pedigree. It does not try to guess the future; it establishes a historical efficiency baseline to show where the consensus market diverges from it.

Picks before Week 5 of 2026 use the engine without the quarterback adjustment.

The data files

Every pick and result on this site is free to download as CSV or JSON, one file per season and one per week.

Reuse them for anything under CC BY 4.0, with credit to The Counter, as the license file says.
